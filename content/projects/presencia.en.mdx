## Problem

Small-group leaders tracked attendance on paper and chat messages, and weekly reports to their pastor took hours to put together.

## What we built

- Native iOS (SwiftUI) and Android (Jetpack Compose) apps sharing one Supabase/PostgreSQL backend, built by a team of five.
- My part: I led the iOS app, chose the name Presencia, set the neumorphic visual style, and built the landing page.
- A meeting-first model: every meeting pre-fills attendance for active members, so a leader records a session in seconds.
- Activity types at three levels (union, church and the group's own), and a deputy flow: a 6-digit code lets a helper record the meeting for 24 hours, and the leader approves it.
- Accounts for members too: their own activities, missionary pairs, Bible studies and Saturday worship attendance.
- A landing page, built by me, with a scroll-driven 3D phone in Three.js and CSS fallbacks.
