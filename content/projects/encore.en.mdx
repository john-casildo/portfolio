## Problem

Musicians who need an instrument for a gig or a semester rarely want to buy one, and owners have gear sitting idle. There was no simple way in Costa Rica to connect the two.

## What I built

- An iOS app (SwiftUI) on Supabase, with one account that can switch between renting and hosting.
- Renters browse by category and nearby location, open an instrument with photos, host and reviews, then book through a four-step checkout: dates, pickup, payment and confirmation.
- Hosts get a dashboard, listings with photo upload, and a reservations queue where they approve or decline requests and block dates on a calendar.
- In-app messaging between host and renter, with the instrument shown in the conversation.
- Full English and Spanish support, plus light and dark themes.
- Designed screen by screen as wireframes first, then built.
